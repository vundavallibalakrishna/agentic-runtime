# GRC roadmap cuts

Decided 2026-11-15 for an offline-first GRC deployment.

These stay optional connectors. They are not part of the GRC pack and are not on its roadmap:

- Salesforce
- HubSpot
- Zendesk

Do not add these until a named offline customer asks, and then as a pack rather than core:

- endpoint detection collectors
- human-resources systems
- regulator feeds

The GRC pack imports the customer's own catalogue text. It does not ship framework prose.
