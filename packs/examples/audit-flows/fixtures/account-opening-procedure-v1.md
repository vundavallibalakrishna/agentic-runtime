# Acme Account Opening Procedure

Version 1.0. Owner: Head of Operations. Effective 1 January 2026.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This procedure covers how a new customer account is opened, from the application to the first transaction. It applies to every branch and to the online channel.

## 2. Customer identification

2.1 Before an account is opened, the Customer Service Officer collects a government photo identity document and proof of address from the applicant.

2.2 The Branch Operations Manager checks every application against the identity documents and signs the application form before the account is activated. An application without this signature must not be activated.

2.3 The account opening system rejects an application when the identity number is already linked to a closed account marked as fraud.

## 3. Transaction profile

3.1 The Customer Service Officer records the customer's declared monthly transaction amount in the transaction profile at account opening.

3.2 An account without a transaction profile must not be activated.

3.3 The Compliance Officer reviews, every month, a report of accounts whose actual monthly transactions exceed the declared amount by more than 50 percent, and records the outcome of each review.

## 4. Screening

4.1 The account opening system screens every applicant against the sanctions list before activation and blocks a match automatically.

4.2 The Compliance Officer reviews blocked applications within two working days.

## 5. Dormant accounts

5.1 An account with no customer transaction for twelve months is classed as dormant.

5.2 Reactivating a dormant account carries a risk of unauthorised use by staff.

## 6. Records

6.1 Account opening documents are kept for five years after the account is closed.

6.2 Internal Audit tests a sample of new accounts every quarter.
