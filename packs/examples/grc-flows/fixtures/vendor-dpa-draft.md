# Data Processing Addendum — CloudNimbus Ltd (Vendor Draft)

## 1. Subject matter and duration
The Processor processes Customer Personal Data only to provide the Services, for the term of the Agreement.

## 2. Instructions
The Processor processes Personal Data only on documented instructions from the Controller.

## 3. Confidentiality
The Processor ensures that people authorised to process Personal Data are bound by confidentiality.

## 4. Security
The Processor implements technical and organisational measures it considers reasonable.

## 5. Sub-processors
The Processor may engage sub-processors at its discretion and will publish a list of sub-processors on its website.

## 6. Data subject requests
The Processor assists the Controller in responding to data subject requests, at the Controller's cost.

## 7. Personal data breach
The Processor notifies the Controller of a Personal Data Breach within 7 days after confirming it.

## 8. Deletion or return
At the end of the Services, the Processor deletes all Personal Data within 180 days.

## 9. Usage analytics
The Processor may use aggregated and de-identified Customer Personal Data to improve its products and for marketing.

## 10. Governing law
This Addendum is governed by the laws of the Cayman Islands.
